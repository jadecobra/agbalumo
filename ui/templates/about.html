The Story

Agbalumo — the network we are building

We built Agbalumo for the times we land in a new city and want food that feels like home, or the African-owned places where we don’t have to explain ourselves. We are creating the network where businesses owned by people like us are seen by people like us, who value them most, and where we can quickly find what we need and plug into the community no matter where we are. Right now the strongest part of this network is Nigerian and West African food in Dallas and a few cities in the Northeast. This is where we started.

Where we are right now

The strongest, most verified part of the network is Nigerian and West African food. Dallas has the best coverage today. We have approved listings with phone and website in a few Northeast cities. Food is the honest wedge — this is where we prove speed and trust first. This is the beginning of the network.
